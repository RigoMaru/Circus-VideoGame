# Stage 01 — The creative direction

## CIRQUE · One More Show
A pocket-sized circus with a big personality. An original browser arcade game inspired by the readable side-scrolling jumps and circus variety of Circus Charlie, rebuilt with tactile 3D toys, warm theatrical lighting, responsive input and contemporary sound.

## The competition brief
Playable game, September 30, 2026 deadline. Repository/files or live link; 60-second gameplay recording or brief text. Prioritize an immediate first play, a memorable look, and a reason to retry.

## Art direction
Vintage travel-poster typography, buttercream paper, vermilion stripes, lagoon teal and warm gold. Sculpted geometric characters with rounded silhouettes. A hand-built miniature big top: striped canvas, bulb garlands, stands, stars and balloons. No stock art or copied game assets.

## Three acts, one readable control language
1. **The Ring of Fire** — ride a toy lion through illuminated hoops.
2. **Roll With It** — balance on a circus ball and leap rolling barrels.
3. **The Grand Finale** — a faster mixed course with higher stakes.

Space / Up / W / tap to jump. X / second touch button to somersault in midair for a landing bonus. Timing matters: no double jump. Short runs, generous collision windows, immediate retries, a seeded daily course, and endless encore mode. Chill mode supplies more hearts and slower speed with a separate record.

## Sound direction
An original swung carnival composition: calliope melody, short brass chords, bouncing bass, brushed/noise percussion, sparkling arpeggios. Musical act variations and responsive jump, ring, trick, damage and finale stingers. Synthesized locally with Web Audio; no network music requests or copied recordings.

## Staged delivery
01 Direction → 02 Blender models → 03 Game loop → 04 Sound → 05 Playtest and submission.
