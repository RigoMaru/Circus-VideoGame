# Stage 04 — “One More Show”

An original composition and procedural arrangement, implemented in `src/audio.js`.

## Arrangement
144 BPM in a major-key carnival idiom, with a 56:44 eighth-note swing. Sixteen original melody bars alternate with an eight-bar harmonic cycle. Triangle-wave calliope carries the melody with a quiet sine overtone. Low triangle bass alternates roots and fifths. Short sawtooth triads provide brass-like offbeats. Pitched sine kick and filtered noise provide the percussion. A short feedback delay adds space and a master compressor controls peaks.

Act 2 transposes the melody up an octave and adds arpeggios; act 3 moves into the higher-energy second melodic phrase. Jump, star, clear, perfect clear, trick landing, damage, act-change, victory and loss have distinct synthesized gestures. Music and effects have independent volume settings. M toggles mute. Pausing suspends audio; resuming starts a new phrase.

## Ownership and delivery
The melody, note arrangement, sound design and implementation were made for this project. No reference video's audio was extracted, sampled or bundled. The piano reference informed the desired cheerful, nimble character, not a copied melody.

Web Audio creates real audio in the browser after a player interaction. There is no external music service, licensing subscription, downloaded track or network requirement. This is intentionally a retro-modern synthesized arrangement, not a live orchestra recording. Browser restrictions can prevent audio before interaction; the game continues silently if audio initialization fails.
